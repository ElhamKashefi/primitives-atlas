# Quantum Algorithmic Primitives Atlas

Version 0.4, 27 October 2026. Edited by Elham Kashefi (University of Edinburgh; LIP6, Sorbonne Université and CNRS).

A card catalogue of quantum algorithmic primitives. Each card gives the primitive's contract in five places
(access, promise, output, error or expressivity, cost), its defining papers, variants, status and limits, and its
partners on the composition map. Companion to the talk "Algorithms as collisions", Nobel Symposium 210, 26 October 2026.

Website: https://elhamkashefi.github.io/primitives-atlas/

Cite as: E. Kashefi (ed.), Quantum Algorithmic Primitives Atlas, version 0.4, 27 October 2026.
