Tell us about your machine

Describe your academic, white-box research system: the most advanced one in your lab today, using information that is already public. We do not ask about any commercial platform, product or company you may be connected to. This is not a benchmark: no scores, no ranking, no comparison of platforms. You do not need to choose primitives: the matchmaking is our job. Every technical question is optional, and a public paper, talk or specification can replace any answer.

How to answer. Tick what your named configuration offers today. Each tick opens a short row: choose a category, and say in a few words how your machine does it (the mechanism, a restriction, or a public example). That is what lets us match the right variant of a primitive. You need not explain every tick: describe up to two distinctive capabilities, and a public reference may replace any detail. An unticked or unexplained item means "?", not "unavailable". Plans go in question 6. The categories carry no numbers and no weights:

  • Native now: part of the configuration, with no added compilation.
  • Routine now: available through a construction your group regards as routine.
  • Substantial overhead now: available, with a cost you would want us to consider.
  • Not in this configuration now: says nothing about future designs.
